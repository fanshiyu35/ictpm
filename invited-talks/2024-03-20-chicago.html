Invitation Letter — Archive Copy

Invitation for Ms. Yanxi Liu to deliver the lecture “Early Results from a Multivariate Ranking Stability Study” at the seminar series of Hawthorn Applied Transport Research, LLC, 20 March 2024, Chicago, United States.

Hawthorn Applied Transport Research, LLC
Suite 900, Lake Street, Chicago, Illinois, United States

Ref. HAR-2024-011
20 March 2024

Ms. Yanxi Liu
National Transport Research Programme (NTRP)

Dear Ms. Liu,

On behalf of Hawthorn Applied Transport Research, LLC, I am pleased to invite you to deliver an invited lecture at our seminar series on 20 March 2024 (Chicago, United States). The proposed lecture, “Early Results from a Multivariate Ranking Stability Study”, has been selected by our programme board for this session.

We have followed your work on multivariate evaluation methods in transport planning with great interest, and we believe your lecture will be of substantial value to our audience of transport researchers, analysts and practitioners.

The host organisation will cover economy-class travel and two nights of accommodation, and offers an honorarium of USD 2,500. We would be grateful for your confirmation at your earliest convenience.

We look forward to welcoming you.

Dr. Nolan Prescott
Director of Research
Hawthorn Applied Transport Research, LLC
