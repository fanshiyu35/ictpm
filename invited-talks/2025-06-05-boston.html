Invitation Letter — Archive Copy

Invitation for Ms. Yanxi Liu to deliver the lecture “Ranking Stability Testing in Standard Practice: Early Adoption Experience” at the seminar series of Bayfront Transit Research Partners, 5 June 2025, Boston, United States.

Bayfront Transit Research Partners
12 Congress Street, Boston, Massachusetts, United States

Ref. BTR-2025-028
5 June 2025

Ms. Yanxi Liu
National Transport Research Programme (NTRP)

Dear Ms. Liu,

On behalf of Bayfront Transit Research Partners, I am pleased to invite you to deliver an invited lecture at our seminar series on 5 June 2025 (Boston, United States). The proposed lecture, “Ranking Stability Testing in Standard Practice: Early Adoption Experience”, has been selected by our programme board for this session.

We have followed your work on multivariate evaluation methods in transport planning with great interest, and we believe your lecture will be of substantial value to our audience of transport researchers, analysts and practitioners.

The host organisation will cover economy-class travel and two nights of accommodation, and offers an honorarium of USD 3,000. We would be grateful for your confirmation at your earliest convenience.

We look forward to welcoming you.

Beatrice Halloran
Executive Director
Bayfront Transit Research Partners
