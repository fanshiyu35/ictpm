Invitation Letter — Archive Copy

Invitation for Dr. Yanxi Liu to deliver the lecture “Delay-Inclusive Appraisal of Station Catchment Schemes” at the Veldhoven Analytics Seminars, 16 March 2023, Eindhoven, the Netherlands.

Veldhoven Urban Analytics
Kennedyplein 40, 5611 ZT Eindhoven, the Netherlands

Ref. VUA-2023-011
16 March 2023

Dr. Yanxi Liu
TransMetric Analytics, Inc.
San Jose, California, USA

Dear Dr. Liu,

On behalf of Veldhoven Urban Analytics, I am pleased to invite you to deliver an invited lecture at the Veldhoven Analytics Seminars on 16 March 2023 in Eindhoven, the Netherlands. The proposed lecture, “Delay-Inclusive Appraisal of Station Catchment Schemes”, has been selected by our programme board for this session.

We have followed your work on multivariate evaluation methods in transport planning with great interest, and we believe your lecture will be of substantial value to our audience of transport researchers, analysts and practitioners.

The host organisation will cover economy-class travel and two nights of accommodation, and offers an honorarium of EUR 1,200. We would be grateful for your confirmation at your earliest convenience.

We look forward to welcoming you to Eindhoven, the Netherlands.

Dr. Femke Bleekrode
Research Lead
Veldhoven Urban Analytics
