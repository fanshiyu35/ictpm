Invitation for Dr. Yanxi Liu to deliver the lecture “Multivariate Ranking of Shared-Mobility Schemes” at the Grönvik Applied Seminars, 19 September 2024, Malmö, Sweden.

Grönvik Mobility Research AB
Stortorget 9, 211 34 Malmö, Sweden

Ref. GVM-2024-044
19 September 2024

Dr. Yanxi Liu
TransMetric Analytics, Inc.
San Jose, California, USA

Dear Dr. Liu,

On behalf of Grönvik Mobility Research AB, I am pleased to invite you to deliver an invited lecture at the Grönvik Applied Seminars on 19 September 2024 in Malmö, Sweden. The proposed lecture, “Multivariate Ranking of Shared-Mobility Schemes”, has been selected by our programme board for this session.

We have followed your work on multivariate evaluation methods in transport planning with great interest, and we believe your lecture will be of substantial value to our audience of transport researchers, analysts and practitioners.

The host organisation will cover economy-class travel and two nights of accommodation, and offers an honorarium of SEK 14,000. We would be grateful for your confirmation at your earliest convenience.

We look forward to welcoming you to Malmö, Sweden.

Dr. Nils Vinterblad
Head of Applied Research
Grönvik Mobility Research AB
