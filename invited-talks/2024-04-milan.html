Invitation for Dr. Yanxi Liu to deliver the lecture “Applying MTRS in Multimodal Network Planning” at the Lambrate Practice Forums, 11 April 2024, Milan, Italy.

Lambrate Mobility Consulting Srl
Via Rombon 11, 20134 Milan, Italy

Ref. LMC-2024-016
11 April 2024

Dr. Yanxi Liu
TransMetric Analytics, Inc.
San Jose, California, USA

Dear Dr. Liu,

On behalf of Lambrate Mobility Consulting Srl, I am pleased to invite you to deliver an invited lecture at the Lambrate Practice Forums on 11 April 2024 in Milan, Italy. The proposed lecture, “Applying MTRS in Multimodal Network Planning”, has been selected by our programme board for this session.

We have followed your work on multivariate evaluation methods in transport planning with great interest, and we believe your lecture will be of substantial value to our audience of transport researchers, analysts and practitioners.

The host organisation will cover economy-class travel and two nights of accommodation, and offers an honorarium of EUR 1,300. We would be grateful for your confirmation at your earliest convenience.

We look forward to welcoming you to Milan, Italy.

Dott. Ing. Tommaso Castelletti
Managing Partner
Lambrate Mobility Consulting Srl
