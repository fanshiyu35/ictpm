Invitation Letter — Archive Copy

Invitation for Ms. Yanxi Liu to deliver the lecture “Staged-Delay-Inclusive Benefit-Cost Analysis: Design and First Applications” at the seminar series of Spreewald Verkehrsforschung GmbH, 16 August 2025, Potsdam, Germany.

Spreewald Verkehrsforschung GmbH
Lange Brücke 5, 14467 Potsdam, Germany

Ref. SVF-2025-042
16 August 2025

Ms. Yanxi Liu
National Transport Research Programme (NTRP)

Dear Ms. Liu,

On behalf of Spreewald Verkehrsforschung GmbH, I am pleased to invite you to deliver an invited lecture at our seminar series on 16 August 2025 (Potsdam, Germany). The proposed lecture, “Staged-Delay-Inclusive Benefit-Cost Analysis: Design and First Applications”, has been selected by our programme board for this session.

We have followed your work on multivariate evaluation methods in transport planning with great interest, and we believe your lecture will be of substantial value to our audience of transport researchers, analysts and practitioners.

The host organisation will cover economy-class travel and two nights of accommodation, and offers an honorarium of EUR 2,200. We would be grateful for your confirmation at your earliest convenience.

We look forward to welcoming you.

Dr. Karen Weidlich
Head of Applied Research
Spreewald Verkehrsforschung GmbH
