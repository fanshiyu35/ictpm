Invitation for Dr. Yanxi Liu to deliver the lecture “Transport Ranking Stability: Five Years of Applied Practice” at the Columbia Ridge Lecture Series, 8 May 2025, Denver, Colorado, USA.

Columbia Ridge Transport Studies
1644 Platte Street, Denver, CO 80202, USA

Ref. CRS-2025-019
8 May 2025

Dr. Yanxi Liu
TransMetric Analytics, Inc.
San Jose, California, USA

Dear Dr. Liu,

On behalf of Columbia Ridge Transport Studies, I am pleased to invite you to deliver an invited lecture at the Columbia Ridge Lecture Series on 8 May 2025 in Denver, Colorado, USA. The proposed lecture, “Transport Ranking Stability: Five Years of Applied Practice”, has been selected by our programme board for this session.

We have followed your work on multivariate evaluation methods in transport planning with great interest, and we believe your lecture will be of substantial value to our audience of transport researchers, analysts and practitioners.

The host organisation will cover economy-class travel and two nights of accommodation, and offers an honorarium of USD 1,800. We would be grateful for your confirmation at your earliest convenience.

We look forward to welcoming you to Denver, Colorado, USA.

Dr. Garrick Calloway
Director
Columbia Ridge Transport Studies
