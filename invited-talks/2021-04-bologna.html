Invitation Letter — Archive Copy

Invitation for Dr. Yanxi Liu to deliver the lecture “Practical Multivariate Assessment of Tram Priority Schemes” at the Sillaro Appraisal Seminars, 15 April 2021, Bologna, Italy.

Sillaro Mobility Consulting Srl
Via delle Lame 73, 40122 Bologna, Italy

Ref. SMC-2021-009
15 April 2021

Dr. Yanxi Liu
TransMetric Analytics, Inc.
San Jose, California, USA

Dear Dr. Liu,

On behalf of Sillaro Mobility Consulting Srl, I am pleased to invite you to deliver an invited lecture at the Sillaro Appraisal Seminars on 15 April 2021 in Bologna, Italy. The proposed lecture, “Practical Multivariate Assessment of Tram Priority Schemes”, has been selected by our programme board for this session.

We have followed your work on multivariate evaluation methods in transport planning with great interest, and we believe your lecture will be of substantial value to our audience of transport researchers, analysts and practitioners.

The host organisation will cover economy-class travel and two nights of accommodation, and offers an honorarium of EUR 1,000. We would be grateful for your confirmation at your earliest convenience.

We look forward to welcoming you to Bologna, Italy.

Dott.ssa Elena Malvasia
Head of Seminars
Sillaro Mobility Consulting Srl
