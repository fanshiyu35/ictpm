Invitation Letter — Archive Copy

Invitation for Dr. Yanxi Liu to deliver the lecture “Staged-Delay Accounting in Benefit-Cost Analysis” at the Sternbach Appraisal Lecture Series, 9 June 2022, Munich, Germany.

Sternbach Institute of Applied Logistics GmbH
Landsberger Strasse 214, 80687 Munich, Germany

Ref. SIA-2022-037
9 June 2022

Dr. Yanxi Liu
TransMetric Analytics, Inc.
San Jose, California, USA

Dear Dr. Liu,

On behalf of Sternbach Institute of Applied Logistics GmbH, I am pleased to invite you to deliver an invited lecture at the Sternbach Appraisal Lecture Series on 9 June 2022 in Munich, Germany. The proposed lecture, “Staged-Delay Accounting in Benefit-Cost Analysis”, has been selected by our programme board for this session.

We have followed your work on multivariate evaluation methods in transport planning with great interest, and we believe your lecture will be of substantial value to our audience of transport researchers, analysts and practitioners.

The host organisation will cover economy-class travel and two nights of accommodation, and offers an honorarium of EUR 1,500. We would be grateful for your confirmation at your earliest convenience.

We look forward to welcoming you to Munich, Germany.

Dr. Ulrich Wendenburg
Director
Sternbach Institute of Applied Logistics GmbH
