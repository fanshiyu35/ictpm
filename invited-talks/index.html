Invited Talks

Eleven invited lectures delivered by Dr. Yanxi Liu between 2019 and 2025, in six countries: Germany, Italy, Japan, the Netherlands, Sweden and the United States.

Each lecture was hosted by an independent private research or consulting organisation as part of its seminar series. An archived copy of each invitation letter is linked below.
