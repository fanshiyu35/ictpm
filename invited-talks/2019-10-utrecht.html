Invitation for Dr. Yanxi Liu to deliver the lecture “Multivariate Tools for Transit Network Evaluation” at the Poldermeer Practitioner Seminars, 3 October 2019, Utrecht, the Netherlands.

Poldermeer Mobility Academy
Maliebaan 88, 3581 CW Utrecht, the Netherlands

Ref. PMA-2019-031
3 October 2019

Dr. Yanxi Liu
TransMetric Analytics, Inc.
San Jose, California, USA

Dear Dr. Liu,

On behalf of Poldermeer Mobility Academy, I am pleased to invite you to deliver an invited lecture at the Poldermeer Practitioner Seminars on 3 October 2019 in Utrecht, the Netherlands. The proposed lecture, “Multivariate Tools for Transit Network Evaluation”, has been selected by our programme board for this session.

We have followed your work on multivariate evaluation methods in transport planning with great interest, and we believe your lecture will be of substantial value to our audience of transport researchers, analysts and practitioners.

The host organisation will cover economy-class travel and two nights of accommodation, and offers an honorarium of EUR 1,100. We would be grateful for your confirmation at your earliest convenience.

We look forward to welcoming you to Utrecht, the Netherlands.

Ir. Maaike Zonnebeld
Programme Director
Poldermeer Mobility Academy
