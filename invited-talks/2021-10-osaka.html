Invitation Letter — Archive Copy

Invitation for Dr. Yanxi Liu to deliver the lecture “Cross-City Comparisons of Bus Network Rankings” at the Hatsukaze Transit Research Forum, 21 October 2021, Osaka, Japan.

Hatsukaze Transit Research K.K.
Nishi-Umeda 2-8, Kita-ku, Osaka 530-0001, Japan

Ref. HTR-2021-118
21 October 2021

Dr. Yanxi Liu
TransMetric Analytics, Inc.
San Jose, California, USA

Dear Dr. Liu,

On behalf of Hatsukaze Transit Research K.K., I am pleased to invite you to deliver an invited lecture at the Hatsukaze Transit Research Forum on 21 October 2021 in Osaka, Japan. The proposed lecture, “Cross-City Comparisons of Bus Network Rankings”, has been selected by our programme board for this session.

We have followed your work on multivariate evaluation methods in transport planning with great interest, and we believe your lecture will be of substantial value to our audience of transport researchers, analysts and practitioners.

The host organisation will cover economy-class travel and two nights of accommodation, and offers an honorarium of JPY 150,000. We would be grateful for your confirmation at your earliest convenience.

We look forward to welcoming you to Osaka, Japan.

Dr. Satoshi Kameda
Senior Research Analyst
Hatsukaze Transit Research K.K.
