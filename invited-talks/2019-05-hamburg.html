Invitation for Dr. Yanxi Liu to deliver the lecture “Ranking Stability in Corridor Investment Decisions” at the Alsterlicht Transport Seminar Series, 16 May 2019, Hamburg, Germany.

Alsterlicht Verkehrsforschung GmbH
Kajen 12, 20459 Hamburg, Germany

Ref. ALT-2019-014
16 May 2019

Dr. Yanxi Liu
TransMetric Analytics, Inc.
San Jose, California, USA

Dear Dr. Liu,

On behalf of Alsterlicht Verkehrsforschung GmbH, I am pleased to invite you to deliver an invited lecture at the Alsterlicht Transport Seminar Series on 16 May 2019 in Hamburg, Germany. The proposed lecture, “Ranking Stability in Corridor Investment Decisions”, has been selected by our programme board for this session.

We have followed your work on multivariate evaluation methods in transport planning with great interest, and we believe your lecture will be of substantial value to our audience of transport researchers, analysts and practitioners.

The host organisation will cover economy-class travel and two nights of accommodation, and offers an honorarium of EUR 1,200. We would be grateful for your confirmation at your earliest convenience.

We look forward to welcoming you to Hamburg, Germany.

Dr. Karen Weidlich
Head of Research Seminars
Alsterlicht Verkehrsforschung GmbH
