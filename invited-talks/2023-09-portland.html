Invitation for Dr. Yanxi Liu to deliver the lecture “Ranking Stability Tests for Regional Rail Corridors” at the Trillium Research Seminars, 28 September 2023, Portland, Oregon, USA.

Trillium Transport Analytics
1021 SW Salmon Street, Portland, OR 97205, USA

Ref. TTA-2023-052
28 September 2023

Dr. Yanxi Liu
TransMetric Analytics, Inc.
San Jose, California, USA

Dear Dr. Liu,

On behalf of Trillium Transport Analytics, I am pleased to invite you to deliver an invited lecture at the Trillium Research Seminars on 28 September 2023 in Portland, Oregon, USA. The proposed lecture, “Ranking Stability Tests for Regional Rail Corridors”, has been selected by our programme board for this session.

We have followed your work on multivariate evaluation methods in transport planning with great interest, and we believe your lecture will be of substantial value to our audience of transport researchers, analysts and practitioners.

The host organisation will cover economy-class travel and two nights of accommodation, and offers an honorarium of USD 1,600. We would be grateful for your confirmation at your earliest convenience.

We look forward to welcoming you to Portland, Oregon, USA.

Dr. Nolan Prescott
Director of Research
Trillium Transport Analytics
