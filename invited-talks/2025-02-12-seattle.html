Invitation Letter — Archive Copy

Invitation for Ms. Yanxi Liu to deliver the lecture “Stability Testing for Regional Rail Corridors” at the seminar series of Tahoma Transit Research Group, Inc., 12 February 2025, Seattle, United States.

Tahoma Transit Research Group, Inc.
Suite 410, Yesler Way, Seattle, Washington, United States

Ref. TTR-2025-006
12 February 2025

Ms. Yanxi Liu
National Transport Research Programme (NTRP)

Dear Ms. Liu,

On behalf of Tahoma Transit Research Group, Inc., I am pleased to invite you to deliver an invited lecture at our seminar series on 12 February 2025 (Seattle, United States). The proposed lecture, “Stability Testing for Regional Rail Corridors”, has been selected by our programme board for this session.

We have followed your work on multivariate evaluation methods in transport planning with great interest, and we believe your lecture will be of substantial value to our audience of transport researchers, analysts and practitioners.

The host organisation will cover economy-class travel and two nights of accommodation, and offers an honorarium of USD 2,800. We would be grateful for your confirmation at your earliest convenience.

We look forward to welcoming you.

Cordelia Marshfield
President
Tahoma Transit Research Group, Inc.
