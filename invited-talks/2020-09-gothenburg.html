Invitation Letter — Archive Copy

Invitation for Dr. Yanxi Liu to deliver the lecture “Robust Ranking Methods for Urban Transit Corridors” at the Granbacken Methods Colloquium, 24 September 2020, Gothenburg, Sweden.

Granbacken Mobility Research AB
Vallgatan 21, 411 16 Gothenburg, Sweden

Ref. GMR-2020-022
24 September 2020

Dr. Yanxi Liu
TransMetric Analytics, Inc.
San Jose, California, USA

Dear Dr. Liu,

On behalf of Granbacken Mobility Research AB, I am pleased to invite you to deliver an invited lecture at the Granbacken Methods Colloquium on 24 September 2020 in Gothenburg, Sweden. The proposed lecture, “Robust Ranking Methods for Urban Transit Corridors”, has been selected by our programme board for this session.

We have followed your work on multivariate evaluation methods in transport planning with great interest, and we believe your lecture will be of substantial value to our audience of transport researchers, analysts and practitioners.

The host organisation will cover economy-class travel and two nights of accommodation, and offers an honorarium of SEK 12,000. We would be grateful for your confirmation at your earliest convenience.

We look forward to welcoming you to Gothenburg, Sweden.

Dr. Lovisa Ekblad
Research Manager
Granbacken Mobility Research AB
