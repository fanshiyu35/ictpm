Invitation for Ms. Yanxi Liu to deliver the lecture “Multivariate Stability Testing for Transit Investment Rankings” at the seminar series of Yunmeng Transport Analytics Co., Ltd., 8 December 2025, online (hosted from China).

Yunmeng Transport Analytics Co., Ltd.
Jiefang Avenue Business Tower, Wuhan, Hubei, China

Ref. YTA-2025-051
8 December 2025

Ms. Yanxi Liu
National Transport Research Programme (NTRP)

Dear Ms. Liu,

On behalf of Yunmeng Transport Analytics Co., Ltd., I am pleased to invite you to deliver an invited lecture at our seminar series on 8 December 2025 (online (hosted from China)). The proposed lecture, “Multivariate Stability Testing for Transit Investment Rankings”, has been selected by our programme board for this session.

We have followed your work on multivariate evaluation methods in transport planning with great interest, and we believe your lecture will be of substantial value to our audience of transport researchers, analysts and practitioners.

The host organisation will cover economy-class travel and two nights of accommodation, and offers an honorarium of CNY 12,000. We would be grateful for your confirmation at your earliest convenience.

We look forward to welcoming you.

Ding Yunsong
Programme Director
Yunmeng Transport Analytics Co., Ltd.
