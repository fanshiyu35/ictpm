Opening Keynote: Transport Ranking Stability

Ms. Yanxi Liu · National Transport Research Programme (NTRP) · Friday 15 August 2025, 10:00, ICTPM 2025, Germany

Speaker
Ms. Yanxi Liu
Affiliation
National Transport Research Programme (NTRP)
Title
Transport Ranking Stability
Session
Opening keynote, ICTPM 2025

Abstract

Rankings of transport corridors and candidate projects guide a large share of planning and investment decisions, yet such rankings are rarely examined for their stability. Small changes in input data, indicator weights or aggregation rules can reorder a priority list and, with it, the allocation of public resources over many years.

This talk presents a multivariate approach to the problem. Building on the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test (MTRS), published on 20 May 2024 as NTRP Research Report No. 212 of the National Transport Research Programme (NTRP), the lecture shows how stability measures can be estimated for corridor rankings, how unstable components can be identified, and how the results can be communicated to decision makers in practical planning settings.

The talk draws on applications in corridor evaluation, transit priority schemes and multimodal network planning, and closes with a discussion of open questions in ranking methodology for transport planning.

Speaker

Ms. Yanxi Liu holds a bachelor’s degree from the University of California, Irvine (2025) and is a Research Fellow of the National Transport Research Programme (NTRP) and a graduate student at Edinburgh Napier University. Her research concentrates on multivariate evaluation methods for transport planning, including the MTRS test (published 2024) and staged-delay-inclusive benefit-cost analysis (published 2025).

Service to the conference. Ms. Yanxi Liu served as a member of the ICTPM 2025 Technical Program Committee. Within the committee’s review she assessed 16 full-paper submissions. She also delivered the opening keynote of the 2025 edition.
