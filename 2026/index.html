ICTPM 2026 — Fourth edition

The fourth edition of the International Conference on Transport Planning and Mobility took place on 18 June 2026.

About the edition

The fourth edition combined keynote lectures, technical sessions on network planning, demand modelling, transit operations and investment appraisal, and the closing best paper award. The programme was prepared by the Technical Program Committee chaired by Prof. Dr. Hendrik Vosskühler.

Technical Program Committee, ICTPM 2026

As a member of the committee, Ms. Yanxi Liu reviewed 25 submissions.
