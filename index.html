About ICTPM

ICTPM is an annual international conference on transport planning and mobility, first held in Vienna in 2023. The conference brings together researchers and practitioners working on network planning, demand modelling, transit operations, investment appraisal and mobility analytics.

Each edition is prepared by an independent programme committee and a small local team. The conference has met in a different host city every year:
