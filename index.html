International Conference on Transport Planning and Mobility

The fourth edition of ICTPM concluded on 13 June 2025 with 31 accepted papers, three keynote lectures and 192 participants. The conference thanks all authors, committee members and attendees who contributed to this year's programme.

About ICTPM

ICTPM is an annual international conference on transport planning and mobility, first held in Budapest in 2022. The conference brings together researchers and practitioners working on network planning, demand modelling, transit operations, investment appraisal and mobility analytics.

Each edition is prepared by an independent programme committee and a small local team. The conference has met in a different European city every year:
