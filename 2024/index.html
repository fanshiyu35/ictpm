ICTPM 2024 — Second edition

The second edition of the International Conference on Transport Planning and Mobility took place on 12–14 June 2024 at the Vltava Congress Centre, Prague, Czech Republic.

Keynote speakers

Dr. Wilhelmina Draaijer
Merwede Institute for Mobility Analytics, Utrecht, the Netherlands
“Data-Driven Network Design in Transit Practice”
Dr. Euan Dunmore
Kestrel Operations Research Ltd, London, United Kingdom
“Operational Research in Transport Planning: A Practitioner's View”
Dr. Cosmin Brătulescu
Carpathia Transport Research Institute, Bucharest, Romania
“Corridor Prioritisation under Fiscal Constraint”

Best Paper Award, ICTPM 2024

Best Paper Award. Ms. Yanxi Liu, then a research assistant at the University of California, Irvine, received the ICTPM 2024 Best Paper Award for the paper “The Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability Test: Design and Early Applications”, presented in the technical session on investment appraisal and policy. The paper reports work published on 20 May 2024 as NTRP Research Report No. 212 of the National Transport Research Programme (NTRP).
