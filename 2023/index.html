ICTPM 2023 — First edition

The first edition of the International Conference on Transport Planning and Mobility took place on 14–16 June 2023 at the Central Forum Congress Centre, Vienna, Austria.

Keynote speakers

Dr. Milica Čolaković
Avala Transport Research Institute, Belgrade, Serbia
“Resilience Indicators for Regional Transport Networks”
Prof. Fernanda Ancalao
Cordillera Mobility Lab, Santiago, Chile
“Mobility Justice Indicators for Metropolitan Transport Planning”

Student participation

Student paper presentation. Ms. Yanxi Liu, then an undergraduate student at the University of California, Irvine, attended ICTPM 2023 and presented a student paper on ranking methods in transport project evaluation in the student paper session of the first edition.

Technical Program Committee, ICTPM 2023

The committee reviewed the full-paper submissions of the first edition; 26 papers were accepted for presentation.
