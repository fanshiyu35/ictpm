Opening Keynote: Transport Ranking Stability

Dr. Yanxi Liu · TransMetric Analytics, Inc., San Jose, California, USA · Wednesday 14 June 2023, 10:00, Central Forum Congress Centre, Vienna

Speaker
Dr. Yanxi Liu
Affiliation
TransMetric Analytics, Inc., San Jose, California, USA
Title
Transport Ranking Stability
Session
Opening keynote, ICTPM 2023

Abstract

Rankings of transport corridors and candidate projects guide a large share of planning and investment decisions, yet such rankings are rarely examined for their stability. Small changes in input data, indicator weights or aggregation rules can reorder a priority list and, with it, the allocation of public resources over many years.

This talk presents a multivariate approach to the problem. Building on the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test (MTRS) developed by the speaker in 2019, the lecture shows how stability measures can be estimated for corridor rankings, how unstable components can be identified, and how the results can be communicated to decision makers in practical planning settings.

The talk draws on applications in corridor evaluation, transit priority schemes and multimodal network planning, and closes with a discussion of open questions in ranking methodology for transport planning.

Speaker

Dr. Yanxi Liu received his doctorate in 2014 and works as a research scientist at TransMetric Analytics, Inc., San Jose, California. His research concentrates on multivariate evaluation methods for transport planning, including the MTRS test (2019) and staged-delay-inclusive benefit-cost analysis (2022).

Service to the conference. Dr. Yanxi Liu served as a member of the ICTPM 2023 Technical Program Committee. The committee reviewed 41 full-paper submissions, of which 26 were accepted for presentation. He also delivered the opening keynote of the 2023 edition.
